HISTORY
  Carpaia grew around Puccio di Spinello's castle and broke free of it through a long struggle and
  a charter signed by the emperor. A heretic was burned in the square; his daughter swore revenge
  and vanished. A three-month siege ended when someone opened a postern by night: no one knows who.

THE THREAT: HELD BY BRIGANDS
  A band of brigands led by Lando Salimbeni has taken over Carpaia: they squeeze the inhabitants,
  hold the notables hostage and use no. 19 (The Mortar Inn) as their headquarters.
  Who is behind it: Lando Salimbeni, leader (Thief 3 · AC 6 [13] · 5hp · THAC0 19 [+0] · ML 10 · AL
  Chaotic): devout, quotes saints in every sentence; wants a safe escort for a precious load;
  secret: is a spy for an enemy town.
  If nobody steps in (one step every 1d4 days)
    1.  They execute a notable in the square.
    2.  Reinforcements arrive: 2d6 more brigands.
    3.  They burn a quarter and leave with the loot.
  Wandering monsters (d6; every 2 turns at night, every hour by day: 1 in 6)
    1.  Lando Salimbeni's brigands: the threat itself, roaming the streets.
    2.  Hippogriff (1d6) · danger 2, HD ~3-4: half eagle, half horse, nesting in abandoned
        towers.
    3.  Giant spider (1d6) · danger 2, HD ~3-4: spiders as big as ponies, weaving webs
        between the pillars.
    4.  Serpent folk (1d6) · danger 2, HD ~3-4: an ancient race of serpents with human
        bodies, devoted to forgotten gods.
    5.  Living statue (1d6) · danger 2, HD ~3-4: a statue of crystal, iron or stone that
        wakes to defend a place.
    6.  Gargoyle (1d6) · danger 2, HD ~3-4: a winged statue that comes alive when no one is
        looking.

COUNTRYSIDE AND ECONOMY
  The country around is core land, fully farmed: about 2,500 peasants live in the surrounding
  6-mile hex, in 10 villages.
  The lord's net revenue: 75 gp a month (900 gp a year), already divided by 2 for the troubled
  times.
  Property for sale
    ·   Inn (500 gp): 5 sp a month net profit (6 gp a year). With 4 servants. Seller:
        Usanna Badnight.
    ·   Large farm (500 gp): 12 gp a month in a good year (a knight's monthly expenses).
        With 3 tenants. Seller: Novella da Messina.
    ·   Large manor (10,000 gp): 75 gp a month; +10 gp a month for each protected village.
        With 8 tenant families and 4d6 families paying homage. Seller: Tancia the Red.
  Rules from Coins and Scrolls (OSR: Land and Investments): land is rarely sold, it is more often
  inherited, given or seized. In times of plague, war or famine every income is halved; in truly
  chaotic times it is divided by 10. Whoever cheats on taxes keeps another 5% of the income, until
  a careful lord audits the books.

  Rumours (d8; T true, F false: referee only)
    1.  Lando Salimbeni's brigands fear fire more than swords. [T]
    2.  The parish's holy water is enough to drive every evil from here. [F]
    3.  Mea Varano (no. 33) is really a noble in disguise. [F]
    4.  Lando Salimbeni's brigands are just a tale to scare children. [F]
    5.  A knight left for the Keep of Spolino a month ago and never came back; his sword
        was worth a castle. [T]
    6.  Hippogriff prowls near the graveyard at night. [T]
    7.  The lord is about to raise taxes to pay for a war. [T]
    8.  In the woods to the north there's a witch who buys hair and teeth. [T]
  Events during the stay (d6)
    1.  A merchant offers a reward for a stolen letter.
    2.  An execution in the square: the condemned swears innocence and names a name.
    3.  Feast of the patron saint: foreign merchants, jugglers, pickpockets everywhere.
    4.  A wedding between two families: everyone is invited, adventurers too.
    5.  Fire alarm at night: everyone to the buckets, and someone takes advantage of the
        chaos.
    6.  A procession blocks the streets; whoever doesn't kneel gets dark looks.
